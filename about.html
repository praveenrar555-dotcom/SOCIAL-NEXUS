What SocialNexus is

SocialNexus is a social app that runs in your browser and can be added to your phone’s home screen. It puts your feed, reels, stories, chats, groups and calls in one place, with an interface that stays quick on an ordinary phone and a slow connection.

What you can do

  • Feed: post text, photos and videos, like, comment, save and share.
  • Reels and stories: short videos, and photos or clips that disappear after 24 hours.
  • Chat and groups: direct messages, group chats and channels, voice notes and photo or video attachments.
  • Calls: voice and video calls, one-to-one and in groups.
  • Explore: find people and hashtags.
  • Control: private accounts, blocking and reporting, and three themes: Dark, Light and Old Film (black and white).

How we think about your data

We collect only what the app needs to work, we do not sell your data, and we show no ads. If you choose to connect Google Drive, your photos and videos are stored in your own Drive and only a link is kept on our side. You can ask for your account and data to be deleted at any time. The details are in the Privacy Policy, the Terms & Conditions and the data deletion page.
