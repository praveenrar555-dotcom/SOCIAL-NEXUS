Fonts

The app uses the fonts already installed on your device (the operating system’s default sans-serif). No font files are downloaded.

Images and icons

  • The app icon (the letter “N” on a blue tile) is original artwork created for this project, self-hosted.
  • The interface icons are Font Awesome Free artwork embedded as SVG (see above). Emoji are drawn by your device’s own emoji font.
  • Photos, videos and audio posted by users belong to those users.

Audio

The in-app sound library plays demo tracks by SoundHelix (soundhelix.com), streamed from their website. They are labelled “SoundHelix demo track” in the app. Before public launch the site owner must confirm that SoundHelix’s current licence allows this use, or replace them with tracks the owner has the rights to.

Call connection services

Calls use free public STUN/TURN relays: Google STUN (stun.l.google.com) and Open Relay by Metered (openrelay.metered.ca).
