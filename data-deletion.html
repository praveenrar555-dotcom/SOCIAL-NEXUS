Delete my data

Delete your account, or ask us to export or correct your data.

Last updated:

You can delete your account and everything linked to it, download a copy of your data first, or ask us to do it for you.

Option 1: delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Log in, open your profile and tap the flip (rotate) button at the top of it.
  2. Optional: press Download My Data to save a JSON copy of your posts, messages, stories, follows and comments.
  3. Press Delete My Account, type DELETE to confirm.

Your account, posts, stories, messages, follows and other data are removed immediately, with no waiting period. This cannot be undone.

Option 2: ask us to delete or export your data

Use this if you cannot log in. The form below opens an email to us; nothing is sent until you press send in your email app, and we keep the request only as long as it takes to handle it.

Files in your Google Drive

If you used Google Drive storage, your photos and videos are in the “SocialNexus” folder of your own Drive. We cannot reach them, so delete the folder there if you want them gone, and remove the app at Google account permissions.

What may remain

  • Messages you already sent stay visible in the other person’s chat only if they still exist there; account deletion removes your messages from our database.
  • Our database provider’s backups keep copies until they are overwritten in the normal cycle.
  • Information we must keep to follow the law or to handle an unresolved abuse report.
