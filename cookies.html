Cookie Policy

What is stored on your device and what needs your consent.

Last updated:

SocialNexus does not use advertising or analytics cookies. This page lists what the app stores on your device and which optional services need your consent.

Your choices

When you first open the app a banner lets you accept or refuse the optional category with equally prominent buttons. You can change your mind at any time using here or the “Cookie settings” link inside the app. Refusing optional services does not stop you from using the app.

Categories

CategoryWhat it doesConsent
EssentialKeeps you logged in, remembers your theme, language and chosen settings, and caches the last posts so the app opens fast and works offline. These are stored in your browser’s local storage (not in tracking cookies).Not required – the app cannot work without them.
Google services (optional)Loads Google’s sign-in script so you can store photos and videos in your own Google Drive. Google may set its own cookies when you connect.Off until you allow it.

How to clear it

Log out in the app, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings. To remove the Google connection also visit your Google account’s app permissions.
