Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and how you stay in control.

Last updated:

This policy explains what SocialNexus (“we”, “the app”) collects, why, who else receives it, and how you can access or delete it. It describes what the app actually does today. We do not sell your data and we do not run advertising or analytics trackers.

1. Who is responsible

The operator named above decides why and how your data is processed (a “data fiduciary” under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and a “controller” for other laws).

3. Legal basis

We process your data because you consented when you created your account (you can withdraw consent by deleting your account) and because it is needed to deliver features you ask for. Optional services, such as Google Drive storage, run only after you opt in.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account data and content: until you delete it or your account.
  • Stories: shown for 24 hours.
  • When you delete your account in the app, your data is deleted immediately. Copies in our database provider’s backups are overwritten on their normal backup cycle.
  • Files in your own Google Drive stay there until you delete them from Drive (we cannot delete files in your Drive for you).

6. Your rights

You can: access and download your data (open your profile, tap the flip button at the top, then Data Export); correct it (edit your profile and posts); delete your account and content (same place: Delete My Account); withdraw consent; nominate someone to exercise your rights; and complain to us or to the Data Protection Board of India. See how to delete your data. If you cannot log in, email us at the address above and we will help. We aim to answer rights requests within 30 days.

7. Children

SocialNexus is for people aged 18 and over. We ask you to confirm your age when you sign up. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if we learn that we have, we delete the account.

8. Security

Passwords, PINs and security answers are hashed. Connections use HTTPS. Access to the database is restricted by row-level security rules. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Cookies and local storage

See the Cookie Policy. You can change your choices at any time with the “Cookie settings” link in the app.

10. Email

We do not collect your email address and we do not send marketing or newsletter emails, so there is nothing to unsubscribe from. If this ever changes, every email will carry a working unsubscribe link and we will ask for your consent first.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects you, we will show a notice in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.

12. Contact and grievances

Questions, requests and complaints: email the contact address or the Grievance Officer above. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days.
